Privacy Policy

PassDrop — Last updated: 28 May 2026

PassDrop is a barcode and QR code manager. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is stored.

What data the app stores

PassDrop stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Barcode and QR code values you scan or enter manually
  • Labels, background colours, and expiry dates you assign to passes

This data is saved using your device's private storage and is not accessible to other apps.

No data leaves your device

PassDrop does not connect to any server. No pass data, usage information, or personal information is ever transmitted, uploaded, or shared with anyone — including the developer.

Camera permission

PassDrop requests access to your camera solely to scan barcodes and QR codes. The camera is only active while the scanner is open. No images or video are stored or transmitted.

No analytics or advertising

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

How to delete your data

You can delete individual passes inside the app at any time. Uninstalling PassDrop removes all stored data from your device permanently.

Children

PassDrop does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date at the top.
